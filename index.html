Two runners. One mark.

Performance wear does not have to look like a billboard, and luxury does not have to sit on the couch. We design in black because it photographs at 5am. We finish in gold because the work deserves a metal, not a neon.

The dual runner is the house mark — two figures in motion, locked in a single stroke. The R monogram is the quiet twin, used where space is scarce.
