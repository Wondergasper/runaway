Athleticism · Leisure · Luxury

A kit for people who will not stay still.

RUNAWAY started from a simple refusal: performance wear does not have to look like a billboard, and luxury does not have to sit on the couch. We design in black because it photographs at 5am. We finish in gold because the work deserves a metal, not a neon.

The dual runner is the house mark — two figures in motion, locked in a single stroke. You will find it on the chest, the thigh, the cap, and the woven hem label. The R monogram is the quiet twin, used where space is scarce.

Materials

Stretch knits with a dry hand. Moisture-wicking finishes. Ventilated mesh where heat builds. Silicone where grip is the point.

Make

Raglan seams for a clean shoulder. Flatlock construction so nothing burns at mile twelve. Gold piping set, not printed.

Use

Train. Travel. Walk the city in the same kit. Cold wash, hang dry. The gold will outlast the season.
